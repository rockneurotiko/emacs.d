Meow's four selection families

They look similar, they behave differently. This is the map.

Meow has four orthogonal ways to build "a selection". They each have their own rules about (a) whether digits after them expand cleanly, (b) whether they leave a real Emacs active region, (c) which verbs act on them. Reading this once removes half of meow's foot-guns.

1. Char / word cursor motions

h t n p w b W B f l

Selection type
cursor-with-hint \u2014 highlighted, but often not an active region.
Digit expansion?
Yes \u2014 w3 selects 3 words.
Region-active?
Not reliably \u2014 hence the d foot-gun.
Best verb
k (kill), which is fallback-safe. Avoid d.

Use for: quick word/char-level edits. Not for: "select a structural unit and act on it" \u2014 use family 2 or 3.

2. Expand-* selections \u2014 the workhorse

e line \u00b7 o block \u00b7 m mark-word \u00b7 M mark-symbol \u00b7 capitals H T N P (expand versions of arrows)

Selection type
expand line / expand block / etc. Explicitly designed to grow.
Digit expansion?
Yes, cleanly \u2014 e4 = 4 lines. This is the family digits were built for.
Region-active?
Yes \u2014 always a real Emacs region. Every verb works.
Best verb
Anything: k c d x ; /.

Use for: "N of X" edits. Counted-line operations. Same-symbol renames within a scope.

3. Things \u2014 structural one-shots

, inner \u00b7 . bounds \u00b7 < to-start \u00b7 > to-end, then a thing-key: r round \u00b7 s square \u00b7 c curly \u00b7 g string \u00b7 p paragraph \u00b7 l line \u00b7 d defun \u00b7 b buffer

Selection type
Structural / bounded. Not an expand-* type.
Digit expansion?
Not the same way as family 2. Don't rely on it for counting. For counted lines use e, not .l.
Region-active?
Yes \u2014 real active region. Verbs work.
Best verb
Anything, especially c (change contents) and x (copy).

Use for: one-shot "select the inside of this string / the whole function / this paragraph". Not for: counted repetition \u2014 use family 2 for that.

4. Secondary selection (the beacon gateway)

G grab \u00b7 R swap-grab \u00b7 X sync-grab

Selection type
A persistent region that stays highlighted while you do other things. Not the primary selection.
Digit expansion?
N/A \u2014 you build it out of a family 2/3 selection first, then G to promote it.
Region-active?
It's its own thing. When your cursor enters it, BEACON state activates automatically.
Best verb
Only i / a / c \u2014 not I / A. See record 0006.

Use for: multi-place edits (beacon workflow). Not for: anything single-shot \u2014 for that, families 2 and 3 are simpler.

The two most confusing overlaps, cleared up

Line: e vs .l

Both "select this line". Use e for anything counted (family 2 \u2014 digits work). .l exists for API symmetry within the thing family but you probably won't reach for it.

Word: w vs m

Both involve a word. w is family 1: moves forward, selects one word, weak region. m is family 2: marks the word under cursor as a real region, plus (in beacon) marks every occurrence. Use m when you want to act on the word; w when you want to traverse.
